# Costs and approvals

This reference can incur charges when deployed. Log Analytics ingestion and retention, Sentinel, and Defender for Cloud are not guaranteed free.

## Defaults

- Sentinel: disabled
- Detection rules: disabled
- Subscription diagnostics: disabled
- Defender paid plans: disabled
- Audit policy: disabled

## Cost review

Estimate daily ingestion and retention requirements; approve Sentinel and Defender costs separately. A Log Analytics daily ingestion cap is not a billing cap. Configure Azure Cost Management budgets and alerts before enabling telemetry. Validate existing subscription plans and diagnostic routes to avoid duplicate charges.

Review incident-data retention, access, and legal hold before any deletion or teardown.
